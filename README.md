### 🎥 Creative Vision & Production Architecture

I direct and produce end-to-end commercial video assets by integrating advanced generative AI systems into traditional post-production and cinema pipelines:

- **Commercial Direction & AI Ad Production:** Conceptualizing, storyboarding, and directing commercial campaigns tailored for viral social distribution, brand identity, and high-conversion ad creatives.
- **Generative Motion & Camera Synthesis:** Directing camera movement, parallax, lighting dynamics, and character consistency using advanced generative video frameworks (**Higsfield AI, Kling AI, Wan, Runway, Luma Dream Machine**).
- **CGI & Hybrid VFX Integration:** Merging generative synthetic footage with 3D camera tracking, matte painting, compositing, visual cleanup, and digital environments.
- **Sonic Architecture & Voice Engineering:** Designing immersive soundscapes, foley, atmospheric audio beds, and neural voice synthesis (**ElevenLabs**) synchronized precisely to visual pacing.
- **Master Post-Production & Color:** Polishing AI generations into cinematic broadcast masters using **Adobe Premiere Pro, CapCut Pro**, custom LUT grading (**Blackmagic Color Science**), and resolution upscaling / motion interpolations.
